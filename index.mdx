The Cubbo Clients API lets you connect your own systems to Cubbo's fulfillment operation. You push orders,
keep your catalog and stock in sync, announce inbound shipments (replenishments), manage returns and receive
webhooks when something changes in the warehouse or during delivery.

The API is a JSON REST API served at:

```text
https://api.cubbo.com/v1
```

Every request except token creation carries a `Bearer` token. See [Authentication](/guides/authentication).

## Test store and production account

Cubbo has a single environment: production. There is no separate sandbox. Instead, you test your integration in a
**test store** before you start operating:

1. **Contact Cubbo.** Write to Cubbo's support team or to your account manager. They guide you through the process
   and create your test store, which Cubbo marks as a test account.
2. **Run all your tests in the test store.** Create the integration and its credentials, configure shipping methods
   and webhooks, and test every endpoint your integration uses.
3. **Activate your production account.** When your tests are complete, ask your account manager to activate your
   production account. It has its own credentials and its own `store_id`.

<Warning>
Test and production accounts never share credentials. When you go live, replace the `client_id`, `client_secret`
and `store_id` in your integration with the production ones.
</Warning>

## Before you start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for your test store">
    Contact Cubbo's support team or your account manager. They guide you and create your test store, with access
    to the Cubbo app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the integration in the app">
    In the Cubbo app, open **Integrations**, create an **Open API** integration and copy your `client_id` and
    `client_secret`. Then map your shipping methods and configure webhooks. See
    [Set up your integration](/guides/setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exchange them for a token">
    Call `POST /v1/auth/token` and use the returned token as a `Bearer` token on every other request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know your store ID">
    Most endpoints take a `store_id`. You find it under **Authorized Stores** when you create or regenerate the
    credentials in the app. One API account can have access to several stores; the API only answers for stores
    it is allowed to see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move to your production account">
    When your tests are complete, ask your account manager to activate your production account. Set up the
    integration there too: it has different credentials and a different `store_id`. Switch them in your
    integration to start operating.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Conventions

- Request and response bodies are JSON (`Content-Type: application/json`).
- Dates and times are ISO 8601 strings in UTC unless the field says otherwise.
- Money fields are decimals in the store's currency.
- Identifiers (`id`, `store_id`, `order_id`) are integers. `order_number` is your own reference and is a string.
- Errors always come back as a JSON object with an `error` key. See [Errors](/guides/errors).
